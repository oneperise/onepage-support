English

One Page includes 365 original pages with reflections and questions and 365 AI-generated images.

All features are free for the first 3 days (72 hours) from the first launch. Afterwards, one non-consumable in-app purchase enables continued use. There is no subscription or automatic charge. The Korean price is KRW 5,900; other storefronts show their local App Store price.

Open the archive and choose Continue reading to purchase, or use the purchase screen after the trial ends. Restore purchases with the same Apple Account. Purchasing and restoring require an internet connection.

Swipe vertically through daily pages, open the reading view for reflections and questions, and save bookmarks and private notes on your device. The archive offers search, saved pages, notes, language and daily reminder settings. Saving images requests add-only Photos permission; sharing uses the iOS share sheet. Check notification permissions and Focus settings if reminders do not arrive.

There is no account or developer-hosted sync. Keep separate copies of important notes before deleting app data. Restore purchases restores access, not deleted personal notes.

Contact wideep98@naver.com with app/iOS versions and reproduction steps. Do not send sensitive notes. For refunds, see Apple’s instructions.
